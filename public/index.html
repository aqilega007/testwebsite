01 // About us

About us

The UNH AI Club is a student-run organization that connects students to real-world AI experiences through workshops, guest speakers, networking, and hands-on projects.

Our mission is to educate, inspire, and empower students to explore every side of AI, from machine learning and language models to vision, robotics, and ethics. Every major and skill level is welcome.
